Scalability for Class Size

PangeaWorld scales from 10 to 300+ students. AI bots backfill unfilled nation and company slots automatically. Even a class of 20 gets a full 8-nation, 80+ company world.

AI-Assisted Learning

AI Advisors &
Planned Hallucinations

PangeaWorld embeds AI as a teaching tool, not just a helper. Students must learn to verify AI output— and the game penalizes them when they don't.

Gemini AI Advisor (Per User)

  • Context-Aware:Grounded in the game's Global Event Ledger — every public action, disaster, trade, and news article
  • Role-Specific Prompts:Executive advisors focus on business strategy; Presidential advisors on macroeconomics and geopolitics
  • Socratic Method:The AI guides thinking with questions — it does NOT give direct optimal answers
  • Sandboxed:Cannot reveal other players' secrets or provide solutions. Strictly partitioned from Confidential Intel Vaults
  • Fully Logged:Every AI interaction is recorded for instructor review and grading

Planned Hallucinations

AI Route Corruption (3% Chance)
When using AI to generate infrastructure routes, there is a 3% chancethe AI intentionally picks an unoptimized path (3–6 extra tiles). If neither the company nor the president catches the error before construction begins, PangeaNews publishes a corruption scandal, devastating approval ratings and company reputation.
Pangea Times Misinformation
Some AI-generated news articles contain deliberate misinformation. Students who rely on headlines without verifying data in their dashboards may make costly decisions based on false intel — teaching media literacy.
Biased Opinion Pieces
The Pangea Times includes opinion pieces with built-in bias. Students learn to distinguish fact from opinion and identify slant in reporting.

Penalties for Not Reviewing AI Work

Route Corruption — Not Caught by Anyone
PangeaNews publishes corruption scandal
President loses 15–25% approval rating
Company loses reputation score
Millions in wasted taxpayer construction costs
Route Corruption — Company Failed, Government Caught
Government rejects the project proposal
Financial penalty applied directly to the company's profit reports
President gains credibility for good governance
Auto-Decision Penalty (Missed Deadline)
Engine applies suboptimal conservative defaults
Pangea Times publishes: "[Company X] appears to be on autopilot…"
Company/nation falls behind competitors

Educational Purpose

These mechanics teach students that AI is a tool, not an oracle. Blindly trusting AI output has real consequences. The grading rubric includes AI interaction quality — instructors review prompts, frequency of use, and whether students critically evaluated AI suggestions.
